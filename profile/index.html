Profile & account

Your account, in your hands.

Your Tyme Boxed profile lives inside the app — that's where your streaks, sessions and settings are. This page shows what's stored and how to change or remove any of it.

Where your profile lives

Open the Tyme Boxed app

Profile → Settings
Everything on your profile — name, email, focus history, device pairing — is edited from inside the app. There is no separate web login.
Get the app → How to manage ↓
What's on your profile

Only what the app needs.

Four things, and nothing beyond them. Your focus data stays yours — we never sell it or hand it to advertisers.

Your identity

A display name and the email address you signed in with. That's the whole account record — no phone number, no address book, no contacts.

Editable in the app, any time

Your focus history

Sessions you've completed, their length, and your streak. This is what makes the progress view work — it's a log of timers, not of what you were doing.

Resettable without deleting your account

Your device & app settings

Which Tyme Boxed device is paired to you, the apps you chose to block, session lengths and notification preferences.

Unpair a device from Settings

What we never store

No screenshots, no keystrokes, no browsing history, no location trail, and no record of the content inside the apps you block.

Detailed in our Privacy Policy
Manage your profile

Three taps, not a form.

Open Tyme Boxed on your phone and everything below is one screen away.

1

Open Profile

Tap the profile icon in the bottom navigation of the Tyme Boxed app.

2

Edit your details

Change your display name, update your email, adjust session lengths, or change which apps get blocked during a session.

3

Manage your device

See the NFC device paired to your account, re-verify it, or unpair it before handing it to someone else.

4

Reset or delete

Clear your focus history from Settings, or remove your account entirely — see the two options below.

Your data rights

Take it with you, or take it down.

Under India's DPDP Act you can ask for a copy of your data or have it erased. Either request goes to the same inbox and a person handles it.

Request a copy

Export my data

We'll send a machine-readable file containing everything tied to your account:

  • Account details (name, email, signup date)
  • Every focus session and streak record
  • Device pairing and app settings
Request export →
Sent within 30 days, to the account email only.
Permanent

Delete my account

Deletion removes your profile and everything listed above from our systems. It cannot be undone.

  • Your profile, sessions and streaks are erased
  • Your device is unpaired and can be set up fresh
  • Order and invoice records are kept only as long as tax law requires
Request deletion →
We confirm from the account email first, then delete within 30 days.
